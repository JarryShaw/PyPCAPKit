## 1.8.1 -- 2026-11-27

A bug-fix release for OSPF, which becomes a versioned family: OSPFv3 is read with its own 16-octet header, and OSPFv1 gets a class and registries of its own. The split is a breaking change, marked **a breaking change to** and ending with how to migrate. Sync Develop pushes `main` into `develop` itself.

- **Added** -- `OSPFv1` reads OSPF version 1 as [RFC 1131](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc1131) lays it out, naming its packet types and AuTypes from the new `pcapkit.const.ospfv1` registries, generated by `pcapkit.vendor.ospfv1`, rather than from OSPFv2's. Its checksum is verified on read under `CHECKSUM_VERIFY` and computed on build, as OSPFv2's is, and its Authentication field is kept as captured ([#3105](https://github.com/JarryShaw/PyPCAPKit/issues/3105)).

- **Changed** -- **a breaking change to** `OSPF`, the abstract base of a versioned family ([#3105](https://github.com/JarryShaw/PyPCAPKit/issues/3105)). A parse gives `OSPFv1`, `OSPFv2` or `OSPFv3` by the Version octet, and a construction by its `version`, which `OSPF.from_fields()` requires where it built OSPFv2. A version with no member, 0 or 4 to 255, is refused: a direct parse raises `ProtocolError`, and from IP protocol 89 the packet is handed on as `Raw`. A parsed layer is an instance of its member, so `layer == 'OSPF'` and `layer == OSPF` are false, while `'OSPF' in frame` and `frame['OSPF']` still find it. Removed with no alias: `pcapkit.protocols.application.ospf.schema` and `.data`, with `ospf_auth_data_selector`; the `Schema_OSPF`, `Data_OSPF`, `Schema_CryptographicAuthentication` and `Data_CryptographicAuthentication` names `pcapkit.protocols.application.ospf` exported; and the base's `read`, `make` and `length`. To migrate, import the OSPFv2 header's schema and data from `pcapkit.protocols.application.ospfv2`, where `Schema_OSPF` is `Schema_OSPFv2`, `Data_OSPF` is `Data_OSPFv2`, the `CryptographicAuthentication` names keep theirs and `ospf_auth_data_selector` is in its `schema`; build with `OSPFv2.from_fields(...)` or pass `version`; and test a layer with `isinstance(layer, OSPF)` or against its member's name, as `layer == 'OSPFv2'`.

- **Changed** -- Sync Develop merges `main` into `develop` with a merge commit and pushes it through the maintenance deploy key, opening a pull request from `sync/main` only when that merge conflicts, where it kept one open, for a person to merge, whenever `develop` lacked `main`'s commits ([#3102](https://github.com/JarryShaw/PyPCAPKit/issues/3102)).

- **Fixed** -- `OSPFv3` reads OSPFv3's own 16-octet header ([RFC 5340 Appendix A.3.1](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc5340#appendix-A.3.1)), with `instance_id` and `reserved` in its info. It was read with OSPFv2's 24-octet layout, so its Instance ID and reserved octet were read as `autype`, the body's first 8 octets as `auth`, and its payload began 8 octets late ([#3105](https://github.com/JarryShaw/PyPCAPKit/issues/3105)).
