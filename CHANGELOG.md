## 1.6.0 -- unreleased

Wave 2 of the roadmap, per the contributing docs' enhancement-proposal page: the protocol pairs that wanted a shared abstract base, and the two that simply had no dissector at all.

- **Added** -- `Radiotap`, a dissector for the fixed 8-octet start of the radiotap header (<https://www.radiotap.org/>) capture tools prepend to an 802.11 frame. `LinuxSLL`/ `LinuxSLL1`/ `LinuxSLL2` dispatch to it for the `ARPHRD_IEEE80211_RADIOTAP` case instead of falling back to `Raw`. Decodes `it_version`, `it_pad` and `it_len`, and walks the present-flags chain (including its bit-31 extended-word chaining) far enough to count its words; the optional-field region those bits select is skipped whole rather than decoded field by field, and the 802.11 MAC frame behind it -- which has no dissector anywhere in this package yet -- is kept as `Raw`. The last of [#2460](https://github.com/JarryShaw/PyPCAPKit/issues/2460)'s five `arphrd_type` follow-ups to get a dissector of its own -- `FRAD` ([#2499](https://github.com/JarryShaw/PyPCAPKit/pull/2499)), `NETLINK` ([#2507](https://github.com/JarryShaw/PyPCAPKit/pull/2507)) and `IPGRE`/`IP6GRE` ([#2501](https://github.com/JarryShaw/PyPCAPKit/issues/2501)) are tracked on their own reviews.

- **Added** -- `GRE`, a dissector for the Generic Routing Encapsulation base header ([RFC 2784](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc2784)) plus the optional Key and Sequence Number extensions ([RFC 2890](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc2890)). `LinuxSLL`/ `LinuxSLL1`/ `LinuxSLL2` dispatch to it for both the `ARPHRD_IPGRE` and `ARPHRD_IP6GRE` cases -- the same GRE framing either way, the "IP"/"IP6" distinction being only which outer IP version the tunnel carries -- instead of falling back to `Raw`. The Protocol Type field dispatches onward as an EtherType, through the same family registry (`Link.__proto__`) Ethernet, VLAN and LinuxSLL already share. One of [#2460](https://github.com/JarryShaw/PyPCAPKit/issues/2460)'s five `arphrd_type` follow-ups to get a dissector (`NETLINK` and `FRAD` are tracked separately on [#2507](https://github.com/JarryShaw/PyPCAPKit/pull/2507) and [#2499](https://github.com/JarryShaw/PyPCAPKit/pull/2499)); `IEEE80211_RADIOTAP` remains `Raw`.

- **Added** -- `Netlink`, a dissector for the fixed 16-octet `nlmsghdr` framing of Linux's own kernel/userspace IPC transport (the kernel's own `netlink(7)` man page; [RFC 3549 Section 2.3.2](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc3549#section-2.3.2)). `LinuxSLL`/ `LinuxSLL1`/ `LinuxSLL2` dispatch to it for the `ARPHRD_NETLINK` case instead of falling back to `Raw`. The byte order the header was written in is ambiguous -- Netlink is kernel/userspace IPC, not wire format -- and is guessed the way Wireshark's own netlink dissector does, by `nlmsg_byteorder()`. Per-family message payloads (routing table entries and the like) and multiple messages packed into one capture are out of scope for this first cut; every message's payload is kept as `Raw`, unconditionally. One of [#2460](https://github.com/JarryShaw/PyPCAPKit/issues/2460)'s five `arphrd_type` follow-ups to get a dissector (`FRAD` is tracked separately on [#2499](https://github.com/JarryShaw/PyPCAPKit/pull/2499)); `IPGRE`/`IP6GRE` and `IEEE80211_RADIOTAP` remain `Raw`.

- **Added** -- `FRAD`, a dissector for Frame Relay's multiprotocol encapsulation ([RFC 2427](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc2427), obsoleting [RFC 1490](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc1490)). `LinuxSLL`/ `LinuxSLL1`/ `LinuxSLL2` dispatch to it for the `ARPHRD_FRAD` case instead of falling back to `Raw`; the direct NLPID and SNAP-encapsulated forms both dispatch onward, to IPv4/IPv6 and (via the EtherType registry every Ethernet/VLAN/LinuxSLL frame shares) ARP among others. The first of [#2460](https://github.com/JarryShaw/PyPCAPKit/issues/2460)'s five `arphrd_type` follow-ups to get a dissector; the other four (`NETLINK`, `IPGRE`/`IP6GRE`, `IEEE80211_RADIOTAP`) remain `Raw`.
