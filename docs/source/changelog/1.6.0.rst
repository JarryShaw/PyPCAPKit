1.6.0 -- unreleased
===================

Wave 2 of the roadmap, per the contributing docs' enhancement-proposal page: the
protocol pairs that wanted a shared abstract base, and the two that simply had
no dissector at all.

* **Added** -- :class:`~pcapkit.protocols.link.netlink.Netlink`, a dissector for
  the fixed 16-octet ``nlmsghdr`` framing of Linux's own kernel/userspace IPC
  transport (the kernel's own ``netlink(7)`` man page; :rfc:`3549#section-2.3.2`).
  :class:`~pcapkit.protocols.link.linux_sll.LinuxSLL`/
  :class:`~pcapkit.protocols.link.linux_sll1.LinuxSLL1`/
  :class:`~pcapkit.protocols.link.linux_sll2.LinuxSLL2` dispatch to it for the
  ``ARPHRD_NETLINK`` case instead of falling back to
  :class:`~pcapkit.protocols.misc.raw.Raw`. The byte order the header was written
  in is ambiguous -- Netlink is kernel/userspace IPC, not wire format -- and is
  guessed the way Wireshark's own netlink dissector does, by
  :func:`~pcapkit.protocols.link.netlink.nlmsg_byteorder`. Per-family message
  payloads (routing table entries and the like) and multiple messages packed into
  one capture are out of scope for this first cut; every message's payload is kept
  as :class:`~pcapkit.protocols.misc.raw.Raw`, unconditionally. One of
  :issue:`2460`'s five ``arphrd_type`` follow-ups to get a dissector (``FRAD`` is
  tracked separately on :pr:`2499`); ``IPGRE``/``IP6GRE`` and
  ``IEEE80211_RADIOTAP`` remain :class:`~pcapkit.protocols.misc.raw.Raw`.
* **Added** -- :class:`~pcapkit.protocols.link.frad.FRAD`, a dissector for Frame
  Relay's multiprotocol encapsulation (:rfc:`2427`, obsoleting :rfc:`1490`).
  :class:`~pcapkit.protocols.link.linux_sll.LinuxSLL`/
  :class:`~pcapkit.protocols.link.linux_sll1.LinuxSLL1`/
  :class:`~pcapkit.protocols.link.linux_sll2.LinuxSLL2` dispatch to it for the
  ``ARPHRD_FRAD`` case instead of falling back to
  :class:`~pcapkit.protocols.misc.raw.Raw`; the direct NLPID and SNAP-encapsulated
  forms both dispatch onward, to IPv4/IPv6 and (via the EtherType registry every
  Ethernet/VLAN/LinuxSLL frame shares) ARP among others. The first of :issue:`2460`'s
  five ``arphrd_type`` follow-ups to get a dissector; the other four
  (``NETLINK``, ``IPGRE``/``IP6GRE``, ``IEEE80211_RADIOTAP``) remain
  :class:`~pcapkit.protocols.misc.raw.Raw`.
