1.6.0 -- unreleased
===================

Wave 2 of the roadmap, per the contributing docs' enhancement-proposal page: the
protocol pairs that wanted a shared abstract base, and the two that simply had
no dissector at all.

* **Added** -- :class:`~pcapkit.protocols.link.radiotap.Radiotap`, a dissector
  for the fixed 8-octet start of the radiotap header
  (<https://www.radiotap.org/>) capture tools prepend to an 802.11 frame.
  :class:`~pcapkit.protocols.link.linux_sll.LinuxSLL`/
  :class:`~pcapkit.protocols.link.linux_sll1.LinuxSLL1`/
  :class:`~pcapkit.protocols.link.linux_sll2.LinuxSLL2` dispatch to it for the
  ``ARPHRD_IEEE80211_RADIOTAP`` case instead of falling back to
  :class:`~pcapkit.protocols.misc.raw.Raw`. Decodes ``it_version``,
  ``it_pad`` and ``it_len``, and walks the present-flags chain (including its
  bit-31 extended-word chaining) far enough to count its words; the
  optional-field region those bits select is skipped whole rather than
  decoded field by field, and the 802.11 MAC frame behind it -- which has no
  dissector anywhere in this package yet -- is kept as
  :class:`~pcapkit.protocols.misc.raw.Raw`. The last of :issue:`2460`'s five
  ``arphrd_type`` follow-ups to get a dissector of its own -- ``FRAD``
  (:pr:`2499`), ``NETLINK`` (:pr:`2507`) and ``IPGRE``/``IP6GRE``
  (:issue:`2501`) are tracked on their own reviews.
