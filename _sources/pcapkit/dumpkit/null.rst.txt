Null Dumper
===========

.. module:: pcapkit.dumpkit.null

:mod:`pcapkit.dumpkit.null` is the dumper for the **NotImplemented** format,
alike those in :mod:`dictdumper`.

.. note::

   This dumper is the fallback used when the given format is not supported. It
   produces no output.
