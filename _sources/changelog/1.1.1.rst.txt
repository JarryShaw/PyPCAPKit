1.1.1 -- 2023-08-15
===================

**Fixed** -- ``AppType`` enumeration lookups; ``typing.TypeAlias`` compatibility
on older interpreters; the output file name chosen by flow tracing; dumping of
``Schema`` objects through a custom dumper; reassembly, broken since 1.0.0
(:issue:`155`). ``Extractor`` now raises an explicit unsupported-call error when
its reassembly or flow-tracing record is read with that feature disabled, and
logs a notice when it enables IPv4, IPv6 or TCP reassembly, or TCP flow tracing.

*Also released:* ``1.1.1.post1``, ``1.1.1.post2`` (through 2023-08-23);
``post2`` renamed the ``strict`` argument of ``Extractor`` and ``extract()`` to
``reasm_strict``, inserting ``reasm_store`` after it, and ``TraceFlow.register``
to ``register_dumper``.
