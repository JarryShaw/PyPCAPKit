1.8.1 -- 2026-11-27
===================

A bug-fix release for OSPF, which becomes a versioned family: OSPFv3 is read with
its own 16-octet header, and OSPFv1 gets a class and registries of its own. The split
is a breaking change, marked **a breaking change to** and ending with how to migrate.
Sync Develop pushes ``main`` into ``develop`` itself.

* **Added** -- :class:`~pcapkit.protocols.application.ospfv1.protocol.OSPFv1` reads
  OSPF version 1 as :rfc:`1131` lays it out, naming its packet types and AuTypes
  from the new :mod:`pcapkit.const.ospfv1` registries, generated by
  :mod:`pcapkit.vendor.ospfv1`, rather than from OSPFv2's. Its checksum is verified
  on read under :data:`~pcapkit.utilities.config.CHECKSUM_VERIFY` and computed on
  build, as OSPFv2's is, and its Authentication field is kept as captured
  (:issue:`3105`).

* **Changed** -- **a breaking change to**
  :class:`~pcapkit.protocols.application.ospf.protocol.OSPF`, the abstract base of
  a versioned family (:issue:`3105`). A parse gives
  :class:`~pcapkit.protocols.application.ospfv1.protocol.OSPFv1`,
  :class:`~pcapkit.protocols.application.ospfv2.protocol.OSPFv2` or
  :class:`~pcapkit.protocols.application.ospfv3.protocol.OSPFv3` by the Version
  octet, and a construction by its ``version``, which ``OSPF.from_fields()``
  requires where it built OSPFv2. A version with no member, 0 or 4 to 255, is
  refused: a direct parse raises
  :exc:`~pcapkit.utilities.exceptions.ProtocolError`, and from IP protocol 89 the
  packet is handed on as ``Raw``. A parsed layer is an instance of its member, so
  ``layer == 'OSPF'`` and ``layer == OSPF`` are false, while ``'OSPF' in frame``
  and ``frame['OSPF']`` still find it. Removed with no alias:
  ``pcapkit.protocols.application.ospf.schema`` and ``.data``, with
  ``ospf_auth_data_selector``; the ``Schema_OSPF``, ``Data_OSPF``,
  ``Schema_CryptographicAuthentication`` and ``Data_CryptographicAuthentication``
  names :mod:`pcapkit.protocols.application.ospf` exported; and the base's
  ``read``, ``make`` and ``length``. To migrate, import the OSPFv2 header's schema
  and data from :mod:`pcapkit.protocols.application.ospfv2`, where ``Schema_OSPF``
  is ``Schema_OSPFv2``, ``Data_OSPF`` is ``Data_OSPFv2``, the
  ``CryptographicAuthentication`` names keep theirs and ``ospf_auth_data_selector``
  is in its ``schema``; build with ``OSPFv2.from_fields(...)`` or pass
  ``version``; and test a layer with ``isinstance(layer, OSPF)`` or against its
  member's name, as ``layer == 'OSPFv2'``.

* **Changed** -- Sync Develop merges ``main`` into ``develop`` with a merge commit
  and pushes it through the maintenance deploy key, opening a pull request from
  ``sync/main`` only when that merge conflicts, where it kept one open, for a
  person to merge, whenever ``develop`` lacked ``main``'s commits (:issue:`3102`).

* **Fixed** -- :class:`~pcapkit.protocols.application.ospfv3.protocol.OSPFv3`
  reads OSPFv3's own 16-octet header (:rfc:`5340#appendix-A.3.1`), with
  ``instance_id`` and ``reserved`` in its info. It was read with OSPFv2's 24-octet
  layout, so its Instance ID and reserved octet were read as ``autype``, the
  body's first 8 octets as ``auth``, and its payload began 8 octets late
  (:issue:`3105`).
