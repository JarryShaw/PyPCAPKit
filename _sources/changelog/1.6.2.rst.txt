1.6.2 -- 2026-11-21
===================

A registry and bug-fix release: the vendor enumerations under :mod:`pcapkit.const`
follow their upstream registries, the ``1.6.1.post1`` refresh included, and closing
a buffered reader works on PyPy 3.12; no dissector changed.

* **Added** -- :class:`~pcapkit.const.tls.extension_type.ExtensionType` names
  ``dane_clientid`` (63), for TLS 1.3 or later, and the temporary
  ``trust_anchors_TEMPORARY_registered_2026_11_18_expires_2027_11_18`` (51764),
  so neither value resolves to ``Unassigned``.

* **Added** -- :class:`~pcapkit.const.reg.linktype.LinkType` names ``ETW2`` (305),
  Event Tracing for Windows messages v2; ``ETW`` (290) is described as v1.

* **Added** -- The app-type tables name TCP port 7151 ``ambesasql`` (AmbesaSQL
  Database Server) and UDP port 8024 ``gaap`` (GAAP Control Packets), and reserve
  each port on the other transport, so
  :class:`~pcapkit.const.reg.apptype.apptype.AppType` resolves neither to
  ``unassigned``.

* **Changed** -- Entry references follow their registries: EPP over QUIC (port
  700) cites :rfc:`10087`, VXLAN (UDP port 4789) the draft revising :rfc:`7348`,
  and the 3GPP entries of :class:`~pcapkit.const.mh.access_type.AccessType`,
  :class:`~pcapkit.const.mh.upn_reason.UpdateNotificationReason` and
  :class:`~pcapkit.const.sctp.payload_protocol_identifier.PayloadProtocolIdentifier`
  the 3GPP contact IANA lists.

* **Fixed** -- On PyPy 3.12, closing a :class:`~pcapkit.corekit.io.SeekableReader`,
  or tearing down an :class:`~pcapkit.foundation.extraction.Extractor` over a
  non-seekable stream, raised :exc:`BufferError`, as slices of the reader's buffer
  view stayed exported until collected. Each slice is released once used
  (:issue:`2806`).
